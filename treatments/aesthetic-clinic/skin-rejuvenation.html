Treatment Overview

What Is Skin Rejuvenation Therapies?

Sun exposure, urban pollution, stress and natural ageing gradually leave skin looking dull or fatigued. Our Skin Rejuvenation Therapies combine clinical skin diagnostics with gentle, targeted in-clinic sessions—such as resurfacing, hydrating infusions or collagen-supporting treatments—chosen specifically for your skin condition.

Clinical Review Note: Every aesthetic treatment plan at OMYRA Beauty, Wellness & Aesthetics Center (Gachibowli, Hyderabad) is individually assessed, reviewed and guided by our qualified clinician. Outcomes vary from person to person depending on biology, skin or hair condition and aftercare.
Potential Benefits

Key Highlights

  • Personalised protocol selected after clinical skin assessment
  • Supports smoother surface feel, balanced hydration and refreshed glow
  • Gentle, progressive approach designed for comfortable recovery
  • Integrates seamlessly with an easy, sustainable home skincare routine

Post-Treatment Care

General Aftercare Guidance

  • Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and reapply when outdoors.
  • Keep skin well moisturised with a gentle, barrier-supporting cream.
  • Avoid harsh scrubs, waxing or strong active serums for 3–5 days post-session.
  • Stay hydrated and follow any specific post-care instructions provided by your clinician.
